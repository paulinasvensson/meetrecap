FAQ

What kinds of input does Meetrecap accept?

You can paste typed notes, upload audio voice memos, or drop in a raw transcript from any meeting platform—Meetrecap handles all three.

How accurate are the action items it generates?

Meetrecap identifies commitments, owners, and deadlines mentioned in your notes or transcript, then lets you quickly review and edit before sharing.

Do I need to change how I take notes?

No. Keep taking notes however you normally do—messy, shorthand, or stream-of-consciousness—Meetrecap does the cleanup for you.

Is Meetrecap built for teams without dedicated meeting-ops staff?

Yes. It's designed specifically for small-to-midsize teams and knowledge workers who need polished recaps without hiring someone to produce them.
