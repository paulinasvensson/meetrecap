FAQ

How messy can my notes be?

As messy as you want. Meetrecap is built to handle fragmented, unstructured, or shorthand notes and still produce a clean, organized summary.

How does it know who owns each action item?

Meetrecap analyzes context and names mentioned in your notes to intelligently assign owners, and you can always adjust assignments before sharing.

Do I need to change how I take notes during meetings?

No. Keep taking notes however works best for you, Meetrecap handles the structuring after the fact so you don't have to change your workflow.

Who is Meetrecap built for?

Remote and hybrid teams, project managers, and consultants who run frequent meetings and need consistent, actionable recaps without spending extra time writing them.
