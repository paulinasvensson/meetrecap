FAQ

What kind of notes can I use?

Any format works—bullet points, transcripts, or stream-of-consciousness notes typed during your meeting.

How accurate are the action items?

MeetRecap's AI identifies commitments and tasks mentioned in your notes with high accuracy, and you can edit before sharing.

Do I need to install anything?

No installation required. Just paste your notes into MeetRecap's web app and get your summary instantly.

Is my meeting data secure?

Yes, all notes are encrypted and never shared or used to train external models.
