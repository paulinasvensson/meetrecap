FAQ

What kind of notes can I use with Meetrecap?

Any format works—bullet points, stream-of-consciousness notes, transcripts, or even voice memo text. Meetrecap is built to handle messy, unstructured input.

How does Meetrecap identify action items?

Our AI scans your notes for commitments, tasks, and decisions, then organizes them into a clear list with owners and deadlines where mentioned.

Do I need to change how I take notes?

No. Meetrecap is designed to work with your existing habits, so you can keep taking notes your way and let the tool handle the cleanup.

Can I share the recap with my team?

Yes, every recap is formatted for easy sharing via email, Slack, or your team's favorite tool with a single click.
